## 💡 Pro Tips for Best PDF Quality

- Paper size: A4
- Margins: Default (or Custom: 2cm all sides)
- Scale: 100%

**Options:**
- ✅ Background graphics (for colors)

- ❌ Page numbers (already in footer)

**Save Settings:**
- Format: PDF

## 📱 Transfer to E-Reader

### After Creating PDFs:

1. **Create folder:** `docs-pdf` (if not exists)

2. **Save all PDFs there** (numbered 01-08)

3. **Connect e-reader via USB**

4. **Copy PDFs to e-reader:**
   - Kindle: Copy to `Documents` folder
   - Kobo: Copy to root or `Books` folder
   - Other: Copy to `Documents` or `Books` folder

5. **Safely eject e-reader**

6. **Open PDFs on your e-reader** - enjoy reading!

→ Reduce quality in print settings  
→ Or create separate PDFs instead of combined
